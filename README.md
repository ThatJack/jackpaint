Simple pygame paint program with full theme customization.<br>

Features:<br>
-Classic drawing tools such as pencil, brush, rectangle, ellipse<br>
-Re-sizable stamps to add to your drawing<br>
-Default nature theme with calming backgrounds and music<br>
-Create your own theme<br>
-Upload your own images to use as backgrounds or stamps<br>
-Upload your own music to play in the background, with controls to change song, play, and pause<br>
-Open images to edit them with the paint program<br>
-Save your work<br>
<br>
Made for a midterm assignment in 2023. Received a mark of 100%.

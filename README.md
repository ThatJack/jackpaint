Simple pygame paint program with full theme customization.

Features:
-Classic drawing tools such as pencil, brush, rectangle, ellipse
-Re-sizable stamps to add to your drawing
-Default nature theme with calming backgrounds and music
-Create your own theme
-Upload your own images to use as backgrounds or stamps
-Upload your own music to play in the background, with controls to change song, play, and pause
-Open images to edit them with the paint program
-Save your work

Made for a midterm assignment in 2023. Received a mark of 100%.
